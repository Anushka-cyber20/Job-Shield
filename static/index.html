Student Anti-Fraud Defense Protocol Safety Advisory

Essential rules and verified government hotlines every student must check before accepting an internship offer.

1

The Golden Rule: Never Pay

No legitimate employer in the world (Google, TCS, Microsoft, startups) will EVER charge a registration fee, laptop insurance deposit, document verification fee, or training kit fee. If they demand money, it is 100% a scam.

2

Corporate Domain Verification

Always inspect the sender's exact email domain after the @ symbol. Authentic recruiters from Amazon use @amazon.com, never @gmail.com or @amazon-hiring-desk.net.

3

Beware Telegram Tasks

Any offer promising daily payouts (₹2,000 - ₹5,000/day) for liking YouTube videos, rating Google Maps listings, or testing apps on Telegram is a criminal prepaid task scheme. They will ask for 'prepaid investments' after paying a small initial bait.

4

Verify Corporate Registration (CIN/MCA)

In India, check if the company is registered on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (mca.gov.in). Look for a valid Corporate Identification Number (CIN) and registered office address before signing agreements.

5

National Cybercrime Helpline

If you or a classmate was defrauded or threatened, immediately call the National Cyber Crime Reporting Hotline: 1930 (India) or file a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. In the US, report to ic3.gov.

6

Never Surrender Original Marksheets

Never submit your original 10th/12th/Degree marksheets or sign heavy bond agreements (>₹25,000 exit penalty) with unaccredited training institutes that promise job guarantees.
